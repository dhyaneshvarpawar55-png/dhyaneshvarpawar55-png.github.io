PaisaTask

A simple task app: complete daily tasks, collect coins, and request a payout.

Open the app

About

PaisaTask lets you sign in with your Google account and complete small tasks such as a daily check-in and viewing sponsored content. Each task adds coins to your balance. Once your balance reaches the minimum, you can send a withdrawal request, which is reviewed manually by the site owner.

How it works

  • Sign in with Google.
  • Complete tasks. Each task has a waiting time before you can repeat it.
  • Collect coins. 100 coins = ₹1.
  • Request a withdrawal once you reach the minimum balance.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what the app needs: your Google account name and email address (for sign-in), and your task activity, coin balance and withdrawal requests, including the payout details you enter (such as a UPI ID).

This data is stored with Google Firebase. We use it only to run the app and process payouts. We do not sell your personal data.

The app may show advertisements from third-party ad networks, which may use cookies or similar technology to show ads. You can ask us to delete your account and data by emailing us.

Terms of Use

  • You must be 18 or older, or have a parent or guardian's permission.
  • One account per person. Fake accounts, automated tools, VPN abuse or any attempt to cheat can lead to a ban and loss of the balance.
  • Coins are not money until a withdrawal is approved. We may change rewards, limits and the minimum payout at any time.
  • Withdrawal requests are checked manually and may be rejected if we find invalid activity.
  • We may stop or change the service at any time.
